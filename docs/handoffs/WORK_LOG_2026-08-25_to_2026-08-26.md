## 12. Overall two-day reconstruction

**What the founder actually worked on.**
On 25 August the work was comprehension and layout: explaining the Gloobal
symbol alphabet and referral IDs inside the app with help sheets, offering two
suggested Gloobal IDs at registration instead of one arbitrary string, marking
shared transactions on the recent lists, and fixing a referral screen that could
not scroll. That last one was a real flexbox defect — a card with
`overflow: hidden` had its automatic minimum height resolved to zero and was
compressed by its flex parent, so the scroll container's content fit exactly and
there was nothing to scroll. On the evening of 25 August and the morning of
26 August the work turned structural: permissions moved from an up-front gate to
just-in-time requests with a visual explainer, every payment now requires a
location fix, payment notifications were added, and the share-leg reference was
re-minted in the Gloobal alphabet so both references on a receipt read the same
way to someone who reads no Latin script.

**What Claude did in response.** On 24 August, inside the CLI, it did a
documented root-cause pass: established which code was deployed before touching
anything, found that Netlify's own `Permissions-Policy` header was disabling the
camera and geolocation for the site itself, found that the settlement engine and
its only caller had disagreed on a function signature so that no settlement had
ever been written, found the QR codec reading a constant defined later in the
concatenated bundle, and removed an INR fallback that made every account resolve
to rupees. It generalised settlement from one corridor to all 194 countries,
seeded the production reference data with explicit founder approval, merged and
pushed, and reported honestly that Render had not deployed. On 25–26 August the
assistant ran in the Claude Desktop app with device file access; it staged and
wrote files into this repository in five batches, and the founder committed them
by hand.

**What reached Git.** Three commits in the audit window: `d148e54` and `261f925`
on 25 August, pushed straight to `main` without a PR, and `2dcab5f` on
26 August, merged through PR #9 as `0065272`. `origin/main` is `0065272`. The
local working copy has not been updated and still shows `261f925`.

26 August automatically. Render went live on the same commit at 07:55:29, but
